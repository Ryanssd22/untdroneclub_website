## VTOL Fixed-Wing Aircraft

The Stingray is a **vertical takeoff and landing (VTOL) fixed-wing plane** built around an existing open-source design with a mostly 3D-printed frame.

The project has been a way for us to get more experience with fixed-wing aircraft, from printing and assembling the airframe to flight testing, repairs, and mission planning.

## What We're Working On

Stingray gives members a chance to get hands-on with the full process of working with a fixed-wing aircraft, including:

* Printing and assembling the airframe
* Setting up electronics and flight controls
* Testing VTOL and forward flight
* Planning practice missions
* Troubleshooting problems after flights
* Repairing and maintaining the aircraft

## Why Stingray?

Unlike some of our other projects, Stingray is not built around one specific competition or final goal.

Instead, we use it for **practice missions and flight testing**. That gives members a chance to make mistakes, fix problems, and get comfortable working with a real aircraft before moving on to more advanced projects.

It also helps us build experience with the differences between multirotors and fixed-wing aircraft, especially when it comes to flight planning, control, and maintenance.

## Goal

The main goal of Stingray is to give members **real experience flying, maintaining, and troubleshooting a VTOL fixed-wing aircraft** so we can carry those lessons into future builds.
